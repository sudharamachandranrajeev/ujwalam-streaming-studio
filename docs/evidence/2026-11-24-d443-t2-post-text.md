# D443 T2: AI post text (evidence, 2026-11-24)

## What
For each post draft made by `packageUploads`, the local model (Qwen3-VL-4B via the T1 runner) gets 4 frames of the clip,
the speech beside the clip if any (`<clip>.transcript.json`, `transcript.json`, `<clip>.srt`, `captions.srt`) and the
detector facts, and answers one json_schema call: title, description, tags, thumbHook, thumbTime, factsSeen. The pure
checker (`services/posting/ai-text-check.mjs`) accepts or rejects it. Any failure keeps the template text and records
`textSource: "template"` and `aiReject: "<code>"` in post.json; success records `textSource: "ai"`, `factsSource`
(detector or model), `tagsDropped`, and `ai {version, thumbHook, thumbTime, factsSeen}`. Prompt and tone live in
`data/posting/ai-text.json` (version 1.0.0). Flag `ai-post-text` (ON, needs `local-ai-model`). One `withModel` per
packaging pass (started at the first draft, stopped after the pass). Never on air (runner refuses; live-check was "off air").

## Checker rules
Schema; description 2-4 lines (one paragraph is split by sentence); 5-12 tags; hook 2-4 words; thumbTime inside the clip;
platform limits from limits.json (title chars, description bytes, tags total, forbidden `<>`), re-checked after composing;
no `#`, `@name`, control or invisible characters, web addresses, or braces other than `{handles.x}`/`{links.x}`;
no profile term (data/profile.local.json, separator and case folded) or chat term; kill counts must equal detector
facts (or factsSeen, source "model"); win/loss words must match the detector result (or factsSeen); numbers: claim-context
numbers must be fact numbers, other digits reject, a tag with a non-fact number is dropped alone; the template description
(links, hashtags) and the D414 music credit must survive in the final description (AI lines go first).

## Gaps
- "Commander" in draft 3 came from the picture; a name read from the screen is not caught by the checker (it knows
  profile terms and chat terms only). Needs a rule or prompt line (T2 follow-up).
- The model wrote "tripple kill" (typo) and one answer said "Kill confirmed" for an unchecked count; style quality is
  small-model quality; facts are checked, spelling is not.
- With no detector facts the model's own facts (kills, victory) were accepted as "model" source; they were not
  independently verified here.
- thumbHook and thumbTime are recorded only; the cover is not changed yet (later step).
- No model-timeout test (covered by the T1 chatJson tests).
