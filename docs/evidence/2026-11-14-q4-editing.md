## Not mirrored, and why

- Press-and-hold pick-up of a row (widget: hold still for the drag time, then drag). The QML list picks a row up after the
  normal drag distance only. Reason: a hold timer would fight the list's own flick; the result of a drag is the same.
- Real mouse and key events are not synthesised in the headless probe. The tests call the same functions the events call
  (one key table drives the `Shortcut` items and the `key:` probe action; one table drives the list's key handler and
  `layer-key:`; `layer-drag:` calls the function the DragHandler calls on release). The drop-line drawing and the
  `DragHandler` itself are therefore not exercised by a test.
- The held "confirm" answer of a nudge (cancelled through `ui/api/cancel`) is coded as in the widget but not tested: the
  nudge refuses while live, which is the only time the server holds a placement change.
- The widget's menu item "Look..." (focus the Look panel) is not present: the Look panel is a later checklist row.
- The layer menu does not call `loadStructure()` after a move (the widget does when scene editing is on); the QML store
  reads the structure when an add window opens.
- Undo and Redo sit in the Layers panel heading, not in a toolbar of the on-air bar (that bar belongs to track A).
